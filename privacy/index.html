Privacy policy 🐾

Last updated: 22 October 2026

The short version: EveryPet stores your email address, the name you choose, and what you log about your pets — so the app can work — plus your purchase status and usage stats you can switch off. No ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell or share your data for marketing. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

EveryPet is an iPhone app for tracking your pet's daily habits. It is run by Daniel Spencer, who is responsible for your information under UK data protection law. You can reach us at everypet@spennyd.co.

What we store

  • Your account — your email address and password. Passwords are stored by our database provider in hashed form; we never see them. We email you a one-time code only if you reset your password or change your email address.
  • Your display name — whatever you'd like the app to call you.
  • Your pets — name, species and avatar, and anything optional you add: birthday or age, breed, microchip number and notes. If you archive a pet or mark them as passed away, we store that too.
  • Your logs — the daily entries you record (food, toilet, water, walks, medication and sickness), including any notes, water amounts and walk lengths.
  • Vet visits — the visits you record: the reason (for example "Check-up" or "Vaccination"), the date and optional time, the clinic or vet's name, what the vet said, and the date and time of any next appointment.
  • Medications and reminders — medication names, doses and how often they're given, plus the medication reminder and daily nudge times you set.
  • App settings — for example, which tips you've already dismissed.
  • Usage information — a few app events: that you created an account, finished a setup step, added a pet (the kind of animal only), saved a log (the type and status, e.g. "Food · Ate well"), saved a vet visit (the reason only), opened the vet summary or turned on a reminder, opened the EveryPet Plus page, that your free days ended, started, bought, cancelled or restored a plan, or a purchase didn't go through (which plan and an error code only, never prices or payment details), and when the app is opened. It's linked to a random account ID and basic device details (iPhone model, iOS version, app version and build, language and region, time zone and screen size) — never your email, your pets' or medications' names, or what you write in notes. We don't record your screen and don't use your location. We use it to fix problems and improve the app. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Purchases — if you buy EveryPet Plus, Apple takes the payment; we receive your purchase status and history (never your card details) so the app knows what to unlock, and to see how EveryPet Plus is doing (for example, how many people choose each plan). We also use when your account was created to work out your three free days.

That's everything. EveryPet has no ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Why we use it

Only to give you the app: keeping your pets' history, building the vet summary you choose to show, sending the reminders you set, and emailing you when you confirm your account or reset your password. The legal basis is that we need this information to provide the service you've asked for. Usage information is different: we use it because we have a legitimate interest in improving the app, and you can switch it off at any time. We look at purchase history for the same reason, to see how EveryPet Plus is doing.

Where it's kept and who helps us

  • Supabase stores your account and everything you log. Row-level security means only your signed-in account can read or change your records.
  • Resend sends our emails (account confirmation, password reset and email change codes), so it handles your email address for that purpose.
  • PostHog collects the usage information described above and stores it in the EU.
  • RevenueCat manages EveryPet Plus purchases with Apple, so it receives your random account ID and purchase history.

These providers may process data outside the UK; where they do, they use the safeguards UK law requires. Your sign-in session is kept in your iPhone's secure keychain. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, so their content never passes through our servers.

We never sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising or marketing.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Delete your account and everything goes with it, straight away. Standard database backups expire on a rolling schedule.

Your rights

  • See and correct — everything we hold is visible in the app. Your account, pets, medications and settings can always be changed there; with EveryPet Plus, your logs and vet visits too. To correct anything else, email us and we'll fix it for free.
  • Delete — Settings → Delete my account permanently erases your account and every record attached to it, immediately, and asks PostHog to delete your usage information and RevenueCat to delete your purchase record too. It doesn't cancel an App Store subscription — do that in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Anything else — for a copy of your data or any other privacy request, email everypet@spennyd.co.
  • If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

EveryPet isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update it here, change the date above, and mention it in the app.
